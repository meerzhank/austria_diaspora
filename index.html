About us

Wherever the mountains meet the Alps

The Kyrgyz Diaspora in Austria is a community organisation supporting Kyrgyz nationals and people of Kyrgyz descent living across the country.

We started as a handful of families meeting for Nooruz in Vienna, and have grown into a network offering practical support, cultural continuity, and friendship between the Kyrgyz and Austrian communities.

Whether you've just arrived or have called Austria home for twenty years, this is a place to find your footing — and to keep a piece of Kyrgyzstan close.

Mission

Building bridges, not just bonds

Promote Kyrgyz culture and traditions throughout Austria.

Support the integration of Kyrgyz families into Austrian society.

Provide practical resources for Kyrgyz nationals settling in Austria.

Foster warm, lasting relations between Kyrgyz and Austrian communities.
